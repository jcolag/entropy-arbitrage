[<i class="fab fa-mastodon"></i>](https://mastodon.social/@jcolag/117399755788806527){: aria-label="Wednesday morning's post on Mastodon with this article" }
[Frederick Douglass Had a Radical Respect for the Constitution](https://jacobin.com/2026/09/frederick-douglass-constitution-slavery-interpretation) from Jacobin

> Constitutional fidelity is not to be blindly adhered to; nor is it merely a pragmatic consideration. The Constitution is truly good when correctly interpreted.

Hashtags:  #USPol

I never quite understood why I keep going back to the speech, so I appreciate this on a personal level for bringing it into focus.  The Constitution and Bill of Rights have a design to prevent power from accumulating.  When we don't try to cheat those ideas, we get the abolition of slavery, extending suffrage, and so forth.  When we look at it as a game to beat, we get anti-government militias with automatic rifles supporting a tyrannical government.

## 9:03 -- Thu 08 October 2026

{% embed https://wagingnonviolence.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/teachers-strike-2019-Iran-.jpg|Teachers hold signs during the March 2019 teachers strike, one of a wave of strikes over pay, privatization and repression|false| %}

[<i class="fab fa-mastodon"></i>](https://mastodon.social/@jcolag/117405423584336598){: aria-label="Thursday morning's post on Mastodon with this article" }
[Iran’s teachers movement perseveres against all odds](https://wagingnonviolence.org/2026/10/iran-teachers-movement-perseveres-against-odds/) from Waging Nonviolence

> Teachers and teachers’ union activists played a prominent role in the January 2026 uprising, when people across the country called for freedom and an end to the Islamic Republic system of government.

Hashtags:  #Iran

Probably not on the blog, because the opportunities don't come by often, but I often talk about how, regardless of the government, the Iranian *people* look a lot like us in the West.  Not only do they descend from an Indo-European culture, but the religious leadership pushed for a baby boom in the late 1960s, meaning that a significant part of the population came of age watching the West globalize and find new ways of expressing ourselves.

Back during the Cold War, people often talked about how we probably could've avoided a lot of conflict by "air-dropping blue jeans and Elvis records" over cities behind the Iron Curtain, and I always got the impression that philosophy applied even more to Iran.  They "wanted their MTV," not religious fundamentalism, and you can see echoes of this in these protest movements.

> Studies show misogynistic peer norms [make men less likely to intervene](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/327819305_Misogynistic_peers_masculinity_and_bystander_intervention_for_sexual_aggression_Is_it_really_just_locker-room_talk) and slower to do it.
