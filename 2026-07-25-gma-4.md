 * Medium:  Paper comic, scanned
 * Length:  Approximately seventy pages
 * Content Advisories:  Plenty of mindless violence

This should go without saying---even though I plan to repeat it with every Book Club installment---but *Content Advisories* do not suggest any sort of judgment on my part, only topics that come up in the work that I noticed and might benefit from a particular mood or head space for certain audiences.  I provide it to help you make a decision, rather than a decision in and of itself.

## Galactic Mutant Animals

The creator describes the as follows.

> Galactic Mutant Animals is a comic I drew when I was twelve (~1993). Probably not the best comic you'll read today, but if somewhere a kid sees it & wants to create its own comic I'll be happy. Sorry you have to speak French to understand all the hilarious jokes.

The creator also provides this biography.

> I draw on my spare time, and love sharing my art under 'libre'/free license ; all my works use CC-BY-SA.
>
> I love monsters.

Occasionally, I see the creator posting new art on Mastodon, which led me here.

## What Works Well?

The art looks much cleaner and more polished.  You can see what drawing the same figures around seventy times does for practice.  And this final story shows a fairly clear path to the artist's more current work.

And while this comes down to taste, I should also point out that I audibly laughed at a couple of the jokes, both the puns such as the Oméganimal and more general jokes such as (see the header) ending the run talking about what to do after the comic ends.

## What Works...Less Well?

{% include lesswell.md %}

If you remember, last week, my pointing out the disjointed feel of the first pages of the final story, we get *much* more of that, instead of the story coming together.  We finish with the slice-of-life bits that could've gone somewhere interesting to the also potentially interesting Oméganimal (and the mutant vegetables come back, but only for a couple of panels as video game characters), but that similarly goes nowhere.  Then it all gets scrapped for *the entirety* of a combat tournament, with entirely new characters.

Speaking of the tournament, constant battle demands careful visual storytelling, really choreographic every aspect of the fight so that we understand what happened.  And this...doesn't do that, and doesn't even seem interested in consistent action.

## Opportunities

I don't see any openings to contribute to or work with the creator, especially not on a comic from over thirty years ago.
