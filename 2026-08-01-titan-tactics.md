## What Works Well?

I almost wrote extensively about the terrible optics of having a seemingly desert-dwelling people, reduced to raiding, described as "savages."  And especially in a game where you send the protagonists to defeat them, because their evil dictator has started building a weapon of mass destruction.  However, it appears that the story did this with intent, and I appreciate that, even if I suspect that many players won't get that far.

The game also plays well enough.  I wouldn't consider it my kind of game, but everything seems smooth and intuitive.

## What Works...Less Well?

{% include lesswell.md %}

The first few levels of the game have a *lot* of "well, as you know," explanations of the rules.  That feels especially peculiar, given how intuitive the game tends to feel.

And at the other end, the game feels unfinished, with a build-up to a final battle, and then...I can't leave the I-assume-penultimate level.
