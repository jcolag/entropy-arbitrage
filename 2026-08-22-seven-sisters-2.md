## What Works Well?

We get most of a complete story, this time through.  And as I may have mentioned last time, you could absolutely imagine this happening in the background of a certain major space opera franchise.

We also have a couple of verses with some interesting ideas.

> Be not afraid  
> Your shell was just a cage  
> You knew the stakes  
> And broke free anyway!

I probably wouldn't worry about getting any allegory on you...

Oh, and a minor point, but for those (like myself) concerned about the bits of the story released under the terms of non-commercial licenses, this album seems to reproduce a lot of that, but released as Free Culture.

## What Works...Less Well?

{% include lesswell.md %}

A couple of sections, especially in *Stellar Showdown*---the big turning point, no less---feels like it hand-waves a *lot* of important details.  We don't get any clarity on the MacGuffin, beyond it having copyable plans, which I can mostly understand.  But we also don't get any insight into the break-in, which seems much more important, and the change in direction happens "off-screen."

## Opportunities

We have the usual Bandcamp-centric means of providing support, such as participating in the community or buying the track(s), not to mention checking out the rest of their work.  Beyond that, I don't see anything.

## What's Adaptable?

We find out that the war has dragged on for eight thousand years.  But we also get a one-off reference to the "Time Un-Dilator" as part of their travel system, and the ship used by the Angels of Aldebaran, the *Blessnaught*.

I don't know if it seems worth referring to other projects, but in addition to resembling a certain story taking place a long time ago, in a galaxy far, far away, the (new?) Christian-influenced references and some structures remind me a lot of the [**Soul Space**]({% post_url 2025-07-05-soul-space %}) role-playing setting.  Do they match up well enough to potentially supplement each other?  I don't know, but the similarity strikes me as interesting.

## Six Years Hence:  Pentagon

I don't think that I have anything useful to say about [**Pentagon**]({% post_url 2020-08-22-pentagon %}).  [The Hill Productions](https://www.thehillproductions.com/home) still maintains their website, but it only points to its YouTube page.  And they did finally release the full version of **Watching Paint Dry (While Eating a Three-Course Meal)** in early July---about a month and a half ago, assuming that you read this when I release it---eight years after releasing the *teaser* for the film, their most recent activity before that, as far as I can find.

I haven't watched their other films, even though **Pentagon** did strike me as a lot of (weird) fun.
